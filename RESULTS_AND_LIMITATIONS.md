# Evidence and Limitations

## Internal synthetic integration

The published *seven-scenario* audit tested four fictional crew members across **28** scenario/person combinations with **168** in-process/API-model checkpoint evaluations. It verified pipeline integration and model reactions to authored disturbances. The later nine-scenario catalog was not part of those exact 28 comparisons.

**Not:** a prospective clinical evaluation, a diagnosis benchmark, or an independent model sensitivity estimate.

## Authentic NASA retrospective datasets

- **OSD-569 / OSD-575**: 8,783 numerical laboratory observations across separate blood assay panels. Of 829 median-only screening flags, **272 (32.8%)** did not remain consistent under preflight-baseline omission. The measurements are correlated within four people; these are **not false-positive rates**.
- **ISS RR-1**: an independently analyzed environmental dataset with three matching engineering measurements. It is not synchronized 17-input, one-second physiology.

NASA records were **not** used to fabricate missing continuous physiological channels or to label synthetic model scores as real-world performance.

## What has not been demonstrated

- No real astronaut medical sensor integration or hardware calibration.
- No validated clinical detection, prognosis, autonomously approved treatment, or emergency classification.
- No medical sensitivity/specificity, AUROC, or proven improvement to health outcomes.
- No real-NASA end-to-end validation of the full synthetic-trained 17-input CNN.
- No flight qualification or certified life-support / EVA alert behavior.

These limitations matter to the scientific interpretation and have been retained deliberately.
