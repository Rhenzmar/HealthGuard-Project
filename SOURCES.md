# Sources and Project Provenance

## NASA sources

- [NASA OSD-569](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-569) — historical Inspiration4 CBC.
- [NASA OSD-575](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-575) — historical Inspiration4 metabolic and other assays.
- [NASA OSDR project](https://osdr.nasa.gov/) — reference and study context.
- [NASA Space Apps 2026 project](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/cnhs-ste/?tab=project).

The detailed internal research repository retains source data hashes, sample/assay identifiers, provenance manifests, methods and reproducibility scripts. This public reader package is intentionally informational; it does not redistribute NASA assay files.

## Software and illustrations

- Interactive anatomy uses third-party 3D anatomy and related assets, with license terms determined by their respective upstream creators; the reader repo includes no anatomy or copyrighted source assets.
- Demo illustrations and all simulated readings are fictional, not photos or measurements from a live NASA mission.
- Browser-generated voice and tones are deterministic demonstration features, not an AI voice clinician.

## Development disclosure

ChatGPT (OpenAI) assisted with programming, analysis, research planning, UI design, debugging, documentation and evaluation preparation. The project team directed scope and remains accountable for its publication and evidence claims.

NASA data access, NASA affiliation and NASA approval are different things: NASA has **not** been claimed to certify or endorse HealthGuard.
